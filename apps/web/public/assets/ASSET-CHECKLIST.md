## Style notes

- Prefer dark UI screenshots for hero and ROI dashboard assets.
- Avoid showing provider logos, API keys, internal dashboards, or tech-stack names.
- Use realistic Bangladeshi business context: e-commerce COD, clinic appointments, tuition follow-up, retail branch calls, EMI/invoice reminders.
- Keep screenshots anonymized: no real phone numbers, customer names, or private data.
- Avoid visuals that only say "AI", "agents", "knowledge", or "campaigns" without a business outcome.
- Use WebP for photos/screenshots, SVG for abstract backgrounds/logos, PNG only when transparency is needed.
